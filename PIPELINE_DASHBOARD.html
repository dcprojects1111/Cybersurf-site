📍 Google Business Profile — MUST DO
⚠ Not Done — Blocking Local Search
When someone in Maroochydore Googles "cybersecurity near me" or "home network security Sunshine Coast" — this is what appears. It's free, drives direct calls and bookings, and Google Reviews become your most powerful sales asset. Nothing else you do in marketing will have this ROI per minute spent.

🔒 No office? No problem — here's exactly how to set it up without showing your address:
1 Go to business.google.com → click Add your business
2 Enter your home address when prompted — Google needs this for verification only. It will NOT appear publicly.
3 On the next screen select "I deliver goods and services to my customers" — this switches you to a Service Area Business
4 Tick "Hide my address" — your street address disappears from the public listing
5 Set your service area — add Sunshine Coast, Noosa, Caloundra, Maroochydore. Your listing shows "CyberSurf Security · Sunshine Coast Region" with no street address.
6 Complete verification — Google will verify you by postcard (sent to your private address) or video call. Takes 1–5 days.
7 Once live: add all services with descriptions + prices, upload logo + headshot, and ask every customer for a Google Review — this is your #1 trust signal.
ⓘ  No map pin will appear (that requires a storefront) but your listing shows your service area shaded on the map. That's fine — your customers are searching by suburb, not looking for a shopfront.
Content Pillars — Every Post Fits One of These Four Buckets
EDUCATE — 40% of content
Plain-English tips, explainers, myth-busting. No direct selling. These are your most shareable posts and build the most trust.
"What actually happens when your password gets breached"
ALERT — 25% of content
Timely responses to current AU scams, breaches, and news. Post within 24 hours of news breaking. Positions you as the local expert.
"New Medicare scam targeting Queenslanders — here's how to spot it"
SHOW YOUR WORK — 20% of content
Behind-the-scenes, anonymised case studies, scan results. Converts curious followers into customers.
"Here's what we found on a Home Security Scan last week"
OFFER — 15% of content
Direct promotion. Never more than 15–20% or you'll lose followers. When you do promote, make the value crystal clear.
"Our $30 Breach Check checks 15 billion records. Book before the long weekend."

Brand Voice Guidelines
✓ Words & Tone to USE
Plain English · "Your data" not "credentials" · "Protect yourself" not "implement security" · Local and personal (mention Sunshine Coast) · Calm and reassuring · "We found..." · "Here's what to do" · "Simple steps" · "No jargon" · Direct and practical
✗ Words & Tone to AVOID
Fear-mongering or panic · Technical jargon (CVE, threat vector, endpoint) · Corporate speak · Competitor names · Overpromising ("100% safe") · ALL CAPS urgency · Vague claims without specifics · "Synergy" / "solutions" / "leverage"
