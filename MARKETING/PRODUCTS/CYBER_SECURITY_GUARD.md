## WHAT THIS PRODUCT IS

CyberSurf's positioning: *"Your cybersecurity expert, on retainer, without the full-time hire."*

A qualified CISO or Security Officer costs $130,000–$170,000/year in salary. A Sunshine Coast medical practice, law firm, or construction company with 8 staff cannot absorb that cost. The Cyber Security Guard is the fractional version — ongoing security management at a price that makes sense for an SME.

**This is not IT support.** IT keeps the lights on. The Guard keeps the doors locked. It works alongside the client's existing IT provider.

## FRACTIONAL SECURITY OFFICER MODEL

The Guard retainer operates as a monthly managed service. Deliverables scale with tier:

### Tier 1 — Guard Essentials (Entry — price TBC, suggest $850/mo)
- Monthly external vulnerability scan and report
- Dark web credential monitoring for all staff emails
- Quarterly MFA audit and remediation guidance
- Breach notification response protocol (Mon–Fri 8am–6pm)
- Email access for questions / quick security advice

### Tier 2 — Guard Standard (price TBC, suggest $1,500/mo)
- Everything in Tier 1
- Monthly phishing simulation (up to 20 staff)
- Quarterly security briefing for staff (30-min Zoom)
- Annual Surf-Check reassessment included
- Priority incident response

### Tier 3 — Guard Strategic (price TBC, suggest $2,500+/mo)
- Everything in Tier 2
- On-site visits (Sunshine Coast — within 2 hours)
- Board/management reporting on cyber risk posture
- Policy and procedure development
- Vendor/supplier security review

**Current status:** Tiers and pricing to be finalised before first client engagement. Do not commit to specific deliverables in any Tier until engagement letter and insurance are in place.

## OPERATIONAL CONSTRAINTS

**Phase 1 capacity ceiling:** 3–5 Guard clients maximum as a sole operator. Each retainer requires:
- Monthly scan and report: ~3–4 hours
- Quarterly review call: ~1.5 hours
- Ad-hoc queries and incident triage: ~2 hours/month average

At 5 Guard clients: ~30 hours/month recurring — sustainable alongside consumer product delivery at current volume.

**Capacity trigger:** At 5 Guard clients, begin formal resource planning. Either sub-contract specific functions (vulnerability scanning, report writing) or consider bringing in a part-time junior analyst.

## LEGAL — ENGAGEMENT LETTER NON-NEGOTIABLE

An engagement letter must be executed before onboarding any Guard client. Minimum clauses:
- Scope of work (exactly what is and isn't covered)
- Non-destructive assessment clause
- After-hours incident response protocol (what's available and what isn't)
- Single-operator acknowledgement — Darryl is the named service provider; no SLA during hospitalisation/absence without a documented backup arrangement
- Liability cap (commensurate with PI insurance coverage)
- Confidentiality
- Data handling and deletion policy
- Termination conditions (30-day notice, minimum)

**Professional Indemnity Insurance:** Must be in force before the first Guard client engagement. PI cover for "Cybersecurity Consultant" category — see `LEGAL/LEGAL_REGISTER.md` for recommended providers and current status.
