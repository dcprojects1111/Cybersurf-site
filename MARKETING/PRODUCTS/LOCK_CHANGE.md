## PRODUCT NAME — WHY "LOCK CHANGE"

The locksmith metaphor is perfect for post-breach psychology. What does every person do after their house key is copied or stolen? They call a locksmith and change the locks. It's the obvious, immediate, non-negotiable next step. No technical explanation needed.

> *"You've just found out someone has your passwords. Now it's time to change the locks."*

Your 52-year-old Noosa dentist gets this instantly. No tech jargon to decode.

| Name | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Lock Change** | **Recommended** | Relatable metaphor, implies action and finality |
| Digital Locksmith Session | Decent | "digital" weakens it |
| Password Vault Setup | Weak | Product-centric, not outcome-centric |
| Credential Reset | Reject | IT helpdesk language |
| Secure Setup Session | Reject | Generic |

## ONLINE-ONLY POSITIONING

### Is it credible?

**Yes — for the 45–54 Sunshine Coast SME audience, online-only is fully credible.** The pandemic normalised video-guided professional services for this cohort. They've done Zoom calls with their accountant, lawyer, and bank since 2020.

The trust gap isn't the format — it's whether Darryl feels like a real local professional or a faceless web service. **That trust comes from:** face on camera, named booking page, Sunshine Coast identity.

**Never say:** "We offer this online only" — sounds like an apology.

**Say instead:**
> *"No driving to an IT shop. No stranger touching your computer. We set it up together — live, on screen — while you watch and learn. Done in 45 minutes."*

This reframes online as:
1. **Convenience** — no travel
2. **Control** — you watch, nothing happens behind your back
3. **Speed** — 45 minutes vs a half-day at a shop

**In-person option:** Not needed at launch. Add if consistent objections received. In-person would justify $149–$199 and targets lower-trust, older segment.

## PITCH FRAME

You are not selling a Zoom call. You are selling **the moment the problem actually gets fixed.**

Post-breach, clients experience *informed helplessness*: they know something is wrong, they don't know how to fix it. Every step they try alone runs into friction — which app, which plan, how to import, what the master password should be.

**That friction is the product.** The $79 session removes it entirely. One call. Done. No tab left open for six months.

### The Pitch — Live
> *"Look, the breach check has given us the data — you've got credentials exposed. The single most effective thing you can do right now isn't technical and it's not expensive. It's getting a password manager set up properly — not downloaded, not half-configured. Done.*
>
> *I run a 45-minute setup session on Zoom. We install Bitwarden, import your existing passwords, set up the browser extension, get your mobile app running, and I walk you through the recovery process so you're not locked out if something goes wrong. You hang up the call with the problem solved.*
>
> *It's $79. Most clients say it's the best $79 they've spent on their digital security — because it actually gets done."*

## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"Can't I just do this myself?"**
> *"Absolutely — and the app is free. Most people also still have it half-set-up three months later. The session isn't the software. It's making sure it actually gets finished in 30 minutes instead of sitting on the to-do list while your credentials stay exposed."*

**"Is $79 just for setting up a free app?"**
> *"The app is free — correct. You're paying for 45 minutes of guided setup, the vault structure that means you don't lock yourself out, and the recovery process being done right the first time."*

**"I already have LastPass / 1Password."**
> *"Good — you've already decided a password manager is worth having. Quick question: is it fully set up? Browser extension on every device, mobile app connected, all passwords imported? If there are gaps, 45 minutes fixes it."*

**"I don't have time this week."**
> *"Understood. To be direct — the longer the gap between finding out your credentials are exposed and changing them, the more exposure you carry. If next week works, I'll hold a slot. What day looks most likely?"*

**"I'm not really that technical."**
> *"That's exactly who this session is designed for. I'll share my screen and you just watch and confirm as we go. There's nothing you need to understand in advance."*
