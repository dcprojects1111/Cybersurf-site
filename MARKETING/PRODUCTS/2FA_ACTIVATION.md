## FUNNEL POSITION

This product sits **after Lock Change** in the natural security sequence. Once a client has changed all their passwords and put them in a vault, the logical next step is locking down the most important accounts with a second factor.

**Natural sales moment:**
> *"You've changed all your passwords and they're safely in your vault. The next thing attackers will try is getting into your email — because if they control your email, they can reset everything else. Let's set up two-factor authentication on your top 5 accounts while we're at it. 30 minutes. $49."*

**Also sells as a standalone** for clients who want account security without a password manager session first.

## ONLINE OR IN-PERSON — BOTH OFFERED

This is the only consumer product with a genuine in-person option. The in-person offering:
- Targets older, lower-tech clients (55+) who are uncomfortable with screen sharing
- Justifies the $49 price point for clients who wouldn't book an online session
- Builds local trust on the Sunshine Coast — word of mouth driver

**Online frame:**
> *"30-minute session on Zoom. We go through your email, banking, and social accounts together. By the end of the call, you've got a second lock on every door that matters."*

**In-person frame:**
> *"If you'd prefer to have me come to you — or meet somewhere local on the Sunshine Coast — I offer in-person sessions as well. Same 30 minutes, same result."*

## WHAT 2FA PROTECTS

Key accounts to lock down (in priority order):
1. Primary email account — controls password reset for everything else
2. Banking / financial institutions
3. Accounting software (Xero, MYOB, QuickBooks)
4. Any platform with saved payment method
5. Business email / Microsoft 365 / Google Workspace
