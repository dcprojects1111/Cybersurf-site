### 1. Who We Are

CyberSurf is an Australian cybersecurity service operated as a sole trader on the Sunshine Coast, QLD. We help individuals and small businesses find out whether their personal information has appeared in known data breaches, and we provide ongoing cyber security support through our Cyber Security Guard service.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we protect it, and what your rights are. It applies to our website, our Basic Breach Check product, and any contact you have with us.

We handle your information in accordance with the **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**.

### 2. What Personal Information We Collect *(APP 3)*

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary for us to do our job.

**Basic Breach Check orders**

| Information | Why we need it |

We do not collect sensitive information (as defined by the Privacy Act 1988) unless you voluntarily include it in a message to us — in which case we treat it with the same care as all personal information.

### 3. The Breach Check Process — Zero Retention of Breach Data *(APP 11)*

This section is important. When we run your Basic Breach Check:

1. Your email address(es) are submitted to a third-party breach intelligence database to check whether they appear in known data breach records.
2. We retrieve the results and prepare your personal report.
3. **We do not store, log, or retain any email/password pair combinations, credential data, or raw breach database records at any point.** The breach data is checked and discarded immediately after your report is generated.
4. Your name, phone number, and email address(es) as submitted by you are retained as part of your order record (see Section 5 below).

We operate a strict zero-retention policy for breach credential data. This is both a security commitment and an ethical one.

### 4. Why We Use Your Personal Information *(APP 6)*

We use your personal information only for the purpose for which it was collected, or a purpose you would reasonably expect. Specifically:

- To deliver your Basic Breach Check report
- To respond to your contact form enquiry
- To send your invoice or receipt
- To contact you about your results, if we have follow-up security information relevant to you
- To comply with our legal obligations (e.g. ATO record-keeping requirements)

We do **not** use your information to sell or share with third-party marketers, build advertising or behavioural profiles, or make automated decisions that affect your rights.

**Direct marketing**

We may occasionally send you information about CyberSurf services related to what you have already purchased (for example, notifying Basic Breach Check customers about our Cyber Security Guard retainer). You can opt out at any time by emailing **privacy@cybersurf.com.au** or clicking unsubscribe in any email we send. We will action all opt-out requests within 5 business days. *(APP 7)*

### 5. How We Store Your Information *(APP 11)*

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access or disclosure.

| Data type | Where it is stored | How long we keep it |
|---|---|---|
| Order records (name, phone, email addresses submitted) | Secure cloud platform | 7 years (ATO tax record obligation), then securely deleted |
| Contact form submissions | Secure email inbox | 2 years, then deleted |
| Breach data / credential pairs from the check | **Not stored — discarded immediately after report generation** | Zero retention |
| Payment records | Held by Stripe only | Per Stripe's privacy policy |

We use access controls, HTTPS encryption for all data in transit, strong passwords, and multi-factor authentication on all systems that hold personal information. Access is limited to CyberSurf personnel only.

When personal information is no longer needed and our retention period has expired, we delete it securely or permanently de-identify it.

### 8. Your Rights — Access, Correction, and Deletion *(APP 12 & APP 13)*

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you
- **Correct** any information that is inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete
- **Request deletion** of your personal information (subject to our legal obligations — for example, we must retain tax records for 7 years under ATO requirements)

To make any of these requests, email us at **privacy@cybersurf.com.au** with your full name and a description of your request. We will respond within **30 days**. There is no charge for making an access or correction request.

If we are unable to give you access to certain information — for example because doing so would affect the privacy of another person, or because we are legally required to retain it — we will explain why in writing.

### 9. Notifiable Data Breaches *(Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), Part IIIC)*

If we become aware of a data breach that is likely to cause you serious harm, we are required by law to:

1. Notify the **Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC)** as soon as practicable after we become aware of the breach; and
2. Notify **you** directly, with a clear description of what happened, what information was involved, and what steps you should take to protect yourself.

Given the nature of our business — handling data breach information on behalf of clients — we treat any suspected security incident as a critical priority. We maintain an internal incident response procedure and will act swiftly if a breach occurs.

### 10. Anonymity *(APP 2)*

Where it is lawful and practicable, you may interact with us without identifying yourself — for example, by browsing our website without submitting any form.

However, for the Basic Breach Check product, we require your name, phone number, and email address(es) to deliver the service. Using a pseudonym or remaining fully anonymous is not practicable for this product.
