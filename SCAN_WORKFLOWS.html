In-Person Pre-Visit Checklist — Send to Customer

Include this in the booking confirmation email so the customer is prepared when Darryl arrives.

  • All devices you want scanned are charged and switched on
  • You know the WiFi password for your home network
  • You have access to your router admin page (usually 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1) — login details are often on the router label
  • If you have more than 3 devices and want them all checked, let Darryl know in advance (add-on applies)
  • If any device has a PIN or password lock, you'll need to unlock it yourself — Darryl will never ask for your passwords
  • Set aside about 2 hours — 30 min per device scan + briefing time
  • Have a pen and paper handy — you may want to take notes during the briefing

⚠ Remote Access Policy — Reinforce This With Every Customer

As a general cybersecurity rule, you should never grant remote access to your computer to someone you don't know personally — even if they claim to be from a legitimate company. This is one of the most common methods used in tech support scams.

CyberSurf's remote scan service is designed so the customer is always in control. We do not use TeamViewer, AnyDesk, or any remote desktop software. The customer runs the scan on their own device and sends us the results. We analyse from our end.

For the in-person service, Darryl is physically present — there is no remote connection involved at any point.

This policy is a marketing differentiator — use it. Most IT companies ask for remote access without a second thought. We don't.
