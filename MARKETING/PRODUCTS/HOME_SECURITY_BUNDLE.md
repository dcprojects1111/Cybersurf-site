## STRATEGIC RATIONALE

The bundle serves two buyer types:

**Buyer Type 1 — The "Get It All Done Now" Client**
This client doesn't want to buy three separate products over three weeks. They want to make a single decision, pay once, and know they're protected. They'll discover all three products exist, compare the combined price, and buy the bundle to feel like they're taking a complete action.

**Buyer Type 2 — The Referred Client**
When a satisfied Breach Check client tells a friend about CyberSurf, the friend often wants to start with something more comprehensive than just the $30 report. The bundle is the "complete starter pack" pitch — *"I paid $129 and got the full setup done in one go."*

## PRICING RATIONALE — $129

- $29 savings is meaningful but not the primary driver — the value is *completion*
- $129 < $150 psychological barrier — stays accessible
- Creates a strong anchor for Dark Web Monitoring upsell: *"You've done the full setup for $129 — the $15/month monitoring keeps it protected going forward."*
- Sets up Cyber Security Guard conversation for any business owners who buy it: *"This covered your personal accounts. If your business email was also in a breach, that's a different conversation."*
