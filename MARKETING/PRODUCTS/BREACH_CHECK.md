## PRICING RATIONALE — $30

| PSM Threshold | Price (AUD) | Interpretation |

## LEGAL RISK SUMMARY

| Activity | Risk Level | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Querying Dehashed with consent | LOW | Explicit consent checkbox at checkout ✅ done |
| Displaying breach names/dates | LOW | Safe ✅ |
| Displaying exposed password (text, secure portal) | MEDIUM | Portal architecture — not in PDF/email |
| Screenshots of Dehashed results | **CRITICAL** | **❌ Do not use** — Copyright Act + APP 6 + statutory tort + ToS |
| Storing breach data >48 hours | HIGH | Zero-retention architecture — delete within 24 hours |
| Operating without Privacy Policy | HIGH | Privacy Policy published ✅ done |

**The three non-negotiables (all completed):**
1. Privacy Policy published ✅
2. Consent checkbox at checkout ✅
3. No screenshots in the report ✅

**New legal risk (2024):** The *Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024 (Cth)* introduced a statutory tort for serious privacy invasions — individuals can now sue directly without an OAIC complaint. Screenshots that capture other breach victims' data trigger this tort from Day 1. Programmatic report format only.
