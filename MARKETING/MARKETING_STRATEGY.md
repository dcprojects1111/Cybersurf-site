---

### ONLINE-ONLY POSITIONING

Your 45–54 Sunshine Coast SME buyer has done Zoom calls with their accountant, lawyer, bookkeeper, and bank since 2020. The format is normalised. The trust gap isn't the channel — it's whether *you* feel like a real local professional.

**Trust signals that close the gap:** Face on camera. Named booking page. Sunshine Coast identity. "A 45-minute session with Darryl" beats "an online setup service."

**Never say:** "We offer this online only" — sounds like a limitation.

**Say instead:**
> *"No driving to an IT shop. No stranger touching your computer. We set it up together — live, on screen — while you watch and learn. Done in 45 minutes."*

This reframes online as: (1) convenience — no travel, (2) control — you watch, nothing happens behind your back, (3) speed — 45 minutes vs a half-day at a shop.

**In-person option:** Not needed at launch. Add if you receive consistent objections. In-person would justify $149–$199 and targets a lower-trust, older segment. Don't build it speculatively.

---

### PRICING — $79

**$79 rationale:**
- 2.6× the $30 entry product — proportionate step up
- Under the $100 psychological barrier — impulse-accessible
- Sold at peak emotional activation (right after breach report delivery)
- At Fogg behaviour model peak: motivation highest it will ever be, friction must be zero

**Anchor play to test in copy:**
> *"Most IT shops charge $180–$250 for a setup visit. This is $79 — done from your desk in 45 minutes."*

**Review trigger:** At 30 sales — if conversion above 15% of breach check buyers, test $99 split. Below 10%, stay at $79.

### PRODUCT NAME — LOCK CHANGE

**Full brand:** CyberSurf Lock Change | **Session name:** The Lock Change Session

The locksmith metaphor is perfect for post-breach psychology. What does every person do after their key is copied? They call a locksmith and change the locks. It's the obvious, immediate, non-negotiable next step. No technical explanation needed.

> *"You've just found out someone has your passwords. Now it's time to change the locks."*

### POST-BREACH PRODUCT SEQUENCE (Sales Executive)

The moment a client receives their breach report, they are in the most receptive state they will ever be. Frame every next step as *clinical care, not a product catalogue.*

| Step | Urgency | Action | CyberSurf Product | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today | Change exposed password everywhere | Lock Change | $79 |
| 2 | Today | Enable MFA on critical accounts | *Free advice — trust builder* | — |
| 3 | This week | Set up Bitwarden properly | Lock Change Session | $79 |
| 4 | Ongoing | Monitor dark web continuously | Dark Web Monitoring | $15/mo |
| 5 | Within 30 days (if business) | Assess business exposure | Surf-Check | $249 |

**The pitch frame:** You are not selling a Zoom call. You are selling **the moment the problem actually gets fixed.** The $79 session removes the friction of a half-finished setup that sits on the to-do list for months.

### OBJECTION HANDLING — LOCK CHANGE

**"Can't I just do this myself?"**
> *"Absolutely — the app is free. Most people also still have it half-set-up three months later with six tabs bookmarked. The session isn't the software. It's making sure it actually gets finished in 30 minutes instead of sitting on the to-do list while your credentials stay exposed."*

**"Is $79 just for setting up a free app?"**
> *"The app is free — correct. You're paying for 30 minutes of guided setup, the vault structure that means you don't lock yourself out, and the recovery process being done right the first time."*

**"I already have LastPass / 1Password."**
> *"Good — you've already decided a password manager is worth having. Quick question: is it fully set up? Browser extension on every device, mobile app connected, all passwords imported? If there are gaps, it's worth 30 minutes."*

**"I don't have time this week."**
> *"Understood. To be direct — the longer the gap between finding out your credentials are exposed and changing them, the more exposure you carry. If next week works, I'll hold a slot. What day looks most likely?"*

**"I'm not really that technical."**
> *"That's exactly who this session is designed for. I'll share my screen and you just watch and confirm as we go."*
