## 2. What the Service Is

The Basic Breach Check is a one-off credential exposure report. You provide us with your full name, your mobile phone number, and up to **two (2) email addresses**.

We query third-party breach databases (including the Dehashed database) to identify whether your email addresses, associated usernames, or related credentials appear in publicly known data breaches. We then compile a report and deliver it to you via a secure, time-limited pCloud link.

The report may include email addresses found in breach data; usernames, passwords (plain-text or hashed), and other credentials associated with those email addresses; and the name and approximate date of the breach in which the data appeared.

**By placing an order, you explicitly consent to receiving a report that may contain exposed passwords and other credential data associated with your email addresses.**

---

## 3. Your Eligibility and Warranties

By placing an order, you confirm that:

**(a)** you are at least 18 years old;

**(b)** you are the **authorised account holder** for every email address you submit — meaning the email account belongs to you, you control it, and you have the right to receive a credential-exposure report for it;

**(c)** you are not submitting anyone else's email address without their explicit, informed written consent and a lawful basis to do so;

**(d)** you will use the report only for the purpose of protecting your own online accounts and credentials; and

**(e)** all information you provide to us is accurate and complete.

**We do not verify account ownership.** If you make a false declaration under clause 3(b) or 3(c), you bear sole legal and ethical responsibility for that, and we may cancel or void your report without refund.

---

## 4. What the Report Is Not

The Basic Breach Check is **not** a comprehensive security audit, **not** a real-time or continuous monitoring service, **not** a guarantee that your credentials are safe, and **not** legal, financial, or professional IT security advice.

**Important — absence from results does not mean your credentials are safe.** Breach databases are incomplete. New breaches occur daily. A nil result means we found no match in the databases we queried at the time of your order — it does not mean your credentials have never been exposed, are not currently exposed, or will not be exposed in the future.
