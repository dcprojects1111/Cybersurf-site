Australian Market Notes

Gap in the Market

Most US-based services (LifeLock, Identity Guard) don't operate properly in Australia — no credit bureau integration, no local support, prices in USD. CyberSurf's advantage is being local, personal, and plain English. No Australian competitor is doing this at the $15–30/month price point for home users.

Compliance Angle for SMB

The Notifiable Data Breaches Scheme (Privacy Act 1988) means businesses that handle personal data have legal obligations. Healthcare, legal, accounting, and real estate firms on the Sunshine Coast are your best SMB targets — they have both the risk and the budget.
