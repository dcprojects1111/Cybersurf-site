## APPROACH: Cold Door / Warm Neighbourhood

---

## TERRITORY / TIMING

**Suburbs:** Buderim, Mooloolaba, Maroochydore, Mountain Creek, Bokarina.
Target streets with established homes 10+ years old — older routers, less tech-savvy owners, higher vulnerability.

**Best times:**
- Tuesday–Thursday: 4:30pm–7:30pm
- Saturday: 9am–12pm
- Avoid Mondays and Fridays

---

## OPENING LINE

You're at the door. They open it. You have 4 seconds.

> *"Hi, I'm Darryl — I'm a cybersecurity guy based here on the Coast. I'm not selling anything today — I'm actually running a launch offer for a handful of homes in this street. Takes about 2 minutes to explain. Is now a bad time?"*

**Why it works:**
- "Not selling anything today" — drops the guard immediately
- "This street" — creates local social proof before you've said anything
- "Is now a bad time?" — easier to say no to than "do you have a minute?" — counterintuitively gets more yeses
- You ARE Darryl. First name only. No business card yet.

---

## FIRST 60 SECONDS

They say it's not a bad time. Now:

> *"So what I do is I scan home networks — routers, connected devices — and I tell you exactly what's visible to the outside world. Most people are shocked. Last week I found open ports on a family home two streets over, firmware that hadn't been updated since 2020, and malware sitting on a shared laptop. The family had no idea.*
>
> *I'm running a launch offer right now — it's called the Complete Home Security Check. For $299 I come to your home, do a full in-person scan of your network and devices, set you up with a password vault, activate two-factor authentication on your key accounts, and run a breach check on your email addresses. Everything done in one week.*
>
> *And for the first 7 people — if the scan finds malware or vulnerabilities, I fix everything before I leave. Same day. That fix session is normally $149 on top. It's included free.*
>
> *I've only got a few spots left. Would that be something worth knowing about your place?"*

**Listen for:** "How long does it take?" = buying signal. "What does it involve?" = buying signal. "We're pretty careful online" = objection, handle below.

---

## TOP 3 OBJECTIONS + EXACT RESPONSES

**1. "We're pretty careful online — I don't think we have anything to worry about."**

> *"That's exactly what the family two streets over thought. The problem isn't what you do online — it's what your router is broadcasting without you knowing. It's completely passive. You don't have to click anything wrong. I can show you in about 90 seconds on my phone what your network looks like from the outside right now. Want to see?"*

Pull out your phone and show them a quick scan result for their street — not their house, their street. That visual closes more doors than any script.

---

**2. "How much does it cost?"**

> *"$299 for everything — breach check, password vault setup, 2FA on your key accounts, and the in-person home scan. If bought separately that's $387. And right now, because I'm just launching, the first 7 people also get the fix session included — that's where I come back and sort out anything the scan finds, malware removed, router locked down, same day. Normally $149 extra. Included free. I've already done [X] of the 7."*

Always state how many spots are gone. Never say "plenty left."

---

**3. "Can I think about it?"**

> *"Of course. Tell you what — I'll leave you something to look at. There's a QR code on there, takes 30 seconds to book. I'll be in this area [day] if you want to catch me then too."*

Hand over the leave-behind. Do not linger. Say: *"Enjoy your evening"* and walk. Lingering kills the sale.

## METRIC TO TRACK

**Appointments booked per 100 doors knocked.**

Industry benchmark for home services: 2–4 per 100 cold.
Target for CyberSurf: 3 per 100 cold, 8–12 per 100 post-job neighbour knock.

Track in a notebook: street, date, doors knocked, conversations had, cards left, appointments booked. After 3 sessions you'll know which suburbs and which times convert.
